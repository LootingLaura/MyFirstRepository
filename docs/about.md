### I'm a digital media designer, performance artist and musician. 

My projects often address psychological and sociocultural issues, aiming to provide an immersive and tangible experience. <br>
I'm particularly interested in the dark, unnoticed spaces within societies, relationships, and networks. The transitional or *liminal spaces* and *backrooms* that, whether we are consciously aware of them or not, have become ingrained in our everyday lives.


### studies 

Since 2023, I'm a student in the Bachelors' *Digital Media* programm at *Hochschule für Künste in Bremen*. <br> 
Previously, I studied *Elementary Music* at Musikhochschule Münster.
